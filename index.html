About Guy

I’ve been performing close-up magic for over a decade.

I started as a teenager, performing for anyone who’d let me. Since then I’ve worked hundreds of weddings, corporate events and private parties across the UK — and the reactions never get old.

In 2026 I won the Comedy Award at The Order of the Magi, Manchester’s magic society. That says a lot about how I work: the magic should leave people baffled, but the whole thing should be fun.

Close-up magic works because it’s personal. There’s no stage, no distance, no screen — just you, your guests and something impossible happening in their own hands.

FAQ

Common questions

What area do you cover?

I’m based in Manchester and cover the whole of the UK. Travel outside the North West may incur a small additional cost — just ask when you get in touch.

How long do you perform for?

A typical booking is two to three hours of mix-and-mingle magic, usually split across the drinks reception and dinner. I’m flexible and happy to work with your timeline.

Do you need any special equipment or space?

No — I perform surrounded, with no stage or props beyond a pack of cards and a few small items. I just need access to your guests.
